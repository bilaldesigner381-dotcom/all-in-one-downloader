Why people use this video downloader and Shorts maker

One tool for every platform

Paste a link from YouTube, Facebook, Instagram or TikTok and the site recognises it automatically. There is nothing to choose and nothing to install.

Download only the part you need

Most YouTube downloaders force you to save the whole video and trim it later. Here you choose the start and end first, so you download seconds instead of minutes of footage.

Pick your video quality

The quality menu shows what each video actually offers, from 144p to 4K, plus Best available. Choose a small file for sharing or full HD for editing.

Turn any video into a Short

Pick a moment of up to {{ max_short }} seconds and get a vertical 1080x1920 video, with a blurred background or a full-screen crop. It is ready for YouTube Shorts, Reels and TikTok.

MP4 video or MP3 audio

Save a clip as MP4, or switch to Audio only and get an MP3 of a song section, a podcast moment or a spoken quote.

Works on phone and computer

No app, no signup and no extension. Open the page in any modern browser, paste the link and download.

How to download a video, a clip or a Short

  1. Paste the linkCopy the video address from YouTube, Facebook, Instagram or TikTok and press Find video.
  2. Choose clip, full video or ShortFor a section, drag the two handles on the timeline or type the start and end time.
  3. Select quality and formatPick a resolution such as 1080p or 720p, or choose Audio only for MP3.
  4. DownloadPress the download button and your file is saved to your device.
